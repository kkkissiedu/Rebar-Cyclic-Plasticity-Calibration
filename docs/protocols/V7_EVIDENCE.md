# V7 evidence ledger: what each component rests on

Rule: every component is either backed by a source that was opened (named below) or is labelled as an HYPOTHESIS of this work, with the test that
supports it. Nothing is presented as established practice unless a source was opened. Updated 2026-11-10; sources marked "opened" were read as full text
(PAPERS folder or the open web); "snippet" means only a search summary was seen.

| Component | Basis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| L1 life: eps_a = eps_f (2 Nf)^alpha (Koh-Stephens form) | Kashani et al. 2019 (opened, Eq. 1 and Tables); Moodley et al. 2024 (opened, Coffin-Manson form); Kashani 2024 review (opened, Eq. 19) | Established form. |
| eps_f and alpha regressed on lambda_p = (L/D) sqrt(fy/100) | Kashani 2019 Eqs. 3 and 4 (opened; the coefficient tables did not extract legibly, so the coefficients are refitted here, not copied); Dhakal and Maekawa's parameter as given in the Kashani 2024 review Eq. 6 (opened): lambda_p = (sqrt(fy)/100)(L/D), fy in MPa (a constant factor different from Kashani 2019's convention; irrelevant here because lambda_p enters linearly) | Established form; coefficients are this work's. Result on development data: a diameter term adds nothing (consistent with Kashani's statement that diameter matters little once buckling governs; he reports it matters for short bars). |
| Life scatter treated as log-normal; accuracy judged in log10 life, factor-of-2 band | ASTM E739 (summary text seen, snippet; the standard states that log life is taken as normally distributed with constant variance and discourages extrapolation outside the tested range) | Standard statistical basis; full standard not opened. Used for the factor-of-2 criterion and for life-uncertainty averaging (Amendment 2). |
| L2 degradation: peak stress normalised by cycle 2 follows a master curve of life fraction n/Nf, shape set by L/D and amplitude | Motivated by the exploratory plot of this work (development set). Cyclic degradation as a function of normalised life is the idea behind damage-based models, but no rebar-specific published form was verified | HYPOTHESIS of this work. Support: cross-validation (beats "no degradation" on 88 % of folds). Three functional forms compared inside folds. |
| Cycle-2 peak stress as a log-linear function of strain amplitude | Standard cyclic stress-strain power law (strain-controlled fatigue practice, ASTM E606 context) | Standard practice, not opened here; used as a fitted empirical relation. |
| L3a core: Chaboche N = 2 with a joint fit and apparent modulus | Hartloper et al. 2021 and de Castro e Sousa et al. 2021 (practice, partly via the authors' code and the open companion paper); series-spring treatment of actuator-based strain: Hajy Akbary et al. 2014 (opened) | Established model family. Fitted here only on L/D 5 bars (Kashani 2019 classes them as not buckled; Moodley 2026 reports asymmetry beyond 3 % strain for L/D 5 in stainless rebar, so this is an approximation). |
| Machine compliance as one stiffness k_m: 1/E_app = 1/E_bar + (pi D / (4 L/D)) / k_m | Series-spring physics (Hajy Akbary 2014 treats the load train as springs in series and finds a load-dependent compliance) | Physical model; the load dependence is NOT represented. Fitted k_m = 215 kN/mm on development L/D 5 bars. |
| L3b buckling distortion, compression half: capacity envelope B (1 + u/u0)^(-q) | Gomes and Appleton 1997 as described in the Kashani 2024 review (opened): post-buckling stress from equilibrium of a buckled bar, proportional to fy/((L/D) sqrt(strain)) (Eq. 3); the same review states the Gomes-Appleton form is not very accurate for L/D < 10 | Mechanics-motivated form, not a derivation: the fitted exponent q (about 0.05 to 1.2) differs from the mechanism value 1/2, so q and u0 are fitted parameters. The coefficient of the closed form was not reconciled between sources and is not used (the envelope strength is solved from the predicted compression peak). |
| L3b tension half: a straightening function back to the tension peak | None found. Dhakal and Maekawa, Kashani and Moodley model the tension branch with Giuffre-Menegotto-Pinto or pinched piecewise-linear branches | HYPOTHESIS of this work (descriptive, arc-position based). Support: cross-validation only; cannot represent variable amplitude. |
| Parameters linear in (1, ln lambda_p, ln amplitude, n/Nf) | Same idea as regressing model parameters on L/D in Moodley et al. 2026 (opened), extended to amplitude and life fraction | Extension of published practice. |
| Transfer to a new bar by material factors on the cycle-2 peak level and on eps_f | No published precedent found (literature survey; Moodley 2026 states its regressions apply only to the same grade and diameter) | HYPOTHESIS of this work. The data available (one steel) can test the machinery, not transfer to a different steel (see V7_PROTOCOL.md). |
| Validation: leave-one-out families, extrapolation blocks, sealed final test, baselines | Consistent with ASTM E739's warning about extrapolation; sealed hold-out is standard model-validation practice | Design of this work, frozen before fitting. |

## Limits that apply to every result
One steel (B500C), one test machine, strain from the actuator LVDT (with machine compliance), constant-amplitude tests to fracture, one specimen per
condition (no scatter measured), diameters 10 to 20 mm, L/D 5 to 15, amplitudes 1 to 6 %. No extensometer-based rebar data exist in the dataset.

## What would make it a stronger research piece
Extensometer-measured tests on a second steel (the Ghanaian bars) to test transfer; replicate specimens to measure scatter; full-text check of ASTM E739,
Dhakal and Maekawa 2002 and Gomes and Appleton 1997 (only the Kashani 2024 review was available for the last two).
